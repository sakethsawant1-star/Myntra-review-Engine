Methodology & Limitations

Research design, data provenance, and known constraints.

Data Collection

Evidence is collected from three sources: Google Play Store Reddit RSS (no API required) YouTube comments

Google Play reviews are fetched via the public-facing scraper (no API key required). Reddit uses public Atom/RSS feeds — subreddits include r/indianfashion, r/Myntra, r/frugalfemalefashion. Reviews are collected in en-IN locale and filtered for keyword relevance before entering the AI stage.

AI Annotation (Gemini Flash)

Each evidence item is sent to Gemini Flash with a 28-dimension behavioral schema (AIAnnotation). The schema captures wishlist intent, frictions (type + severity 1-3), purchase stage, off-platform behavior, and supporting exact quotes (spans). All extracted spans are validated against the source text.

  • Research-neutral: the model is instructed not to assume any friction is the "expected answer"
  • Confidence calibration: 3 = explicitly stated, 2 = implied, 1 = weak inference
  • Contradictions are explicitly noted in contradictory_signal

7-Factor Opportunity Scoring

Each behavioral hypothesis is scored on 7 equally-weighted factors, each expressed as a 0.0–1.0 score:

  • Frequency: Volume of evidence items mentioning this friction
  • Severity: Average friction severity (1-3 scale) normalized to 0–1
  • Purchase Intent: Co-occurrence rate with high_purchase_intent signals
  • Conversion Relevance: Direct link between friction and conversion failure
  • Source Convergence: Whether multiple data sources signal the same friction
  • Segment Concentration: How concentrated this friction is within a specific segment
  • Evidence Confidence: Average AI confidence score (1-3, normalized)

Known Limitations

  • Public reviews skew toward negative experiences (negativity bias in review corpora)
  • Reddit sample may over-represent active online communities, not typical Myntra shoppers
  • Gemini Flash free tier limits batch throughput — large corpora require overnight runs
  • Demographic inference is explicitly prohibited — no gender, age, or income assumed
  • Evidence is observational, not causal — correlations do not imply feature recommendations
